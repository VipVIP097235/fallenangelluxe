☀️ The Eclipse Lore

The Light Within the Shadow
"Even when grounded by sadness or self-doubt... you are still walking, still standing, and still royalty."

🌌 I. The Sun’s Eclipse

Once a guardian of pure celestial light, a great eclipse brought a deep, overwhelming exhaustion. Disconnected from the flawless, shining light around her, she felt like the only one who didn't belong in the heavens.

🪽 II. The Fall & The Insecurity

Feeling flawed compared to the perfect beings above, she cast herself down to Earth. She covered herself in wide sunhats, soft layers, and delicate lace—not just for fashion, but as protective armor because she struggled to look at her own reflection.

🌻 III. The Golden Realization

Falling to Earth didn't ruin her—it transformed her. Golden sun-rays absorbed into her trim, her hair, and her warm, honeyed tones. Her red-bottom heels represent the ground she now walks on: proof that surviving the dark eclipse makes you stronger than remaining untouched in the sky.
